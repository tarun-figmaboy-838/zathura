# DRIFTBOUND: design plan

A browser space adventure that teaches angles. You captain a gunship through a black hole and across
12 levels; every enemy is an angle question, and your answer is literally the angle your cannon turns.

Built with **Three.js** (rendering and game, ES modules, no build step) and **headless Blender**
(every 3D model is generated by a Python script). UI art is made in Figma.

---

## 1. Pillars

| Pillar | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **The math is the weapon** | The answer is the angle the cannon turns. Right lands on the target, wrong visibly misses. |
| **Learn by seeing** | A clean radar diagram per question; the rule appears when you miss, the working when you hit. |
| **Cinematic space** | Real-looking galaxies and stars, a lensed black hole, asteroid journeys, drone camera shots. |
| **Explain everything** | Each new thing (enemy, radar, rule, buttons, boss) is introduced the first time it appears in a visit. |
| **Minimal UI** | No clutter: narration, one question card with the answers, enemy comms, score and pause, each in its own slot. |
| **Fun enemies** | Comic comms banter: raiders with silly callsigns taunt, mock and panic; the Warden boasts. |

## 2. The run

1. **Title**: first-person on the bridge; the live scene through the windshield; Play in the console.
2. **Launch**: the camera leaves the cockpit; the ship is released from the Haven-9 station.
3. **Black hole**: the ship dives in (redshift, time dilation, infalling matter) and emerges beyond.
4. **12 levels** from *Angle Hunt* (angle-game.vercel.app), in three sectors of four:
   - before every level: a short playable journey (steer, shoot rocks and space junk), ending in a portal
   - the level: one raider flies in and holds position; answer its angle question to destroy it
   - end of each sector: a meteor field (aim and fire), then the sector report
5. **The Warden** (boss): 4 turrets, then its core (3 hits), every target an angle question.
6. **Ending**: the ship jumps home; the victory screen shows stars for every sector.

## 3. Levels (Angle Hunt)

| Level | Known angle | Rule | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 76° | Linear pair | 104° |
| L2 | 68° | Vertically opposite | 68° |
| L3 | 118° | Linear pair | 62° |
| L4 | 43° | Linear pair | 137° |
| L5 | 113° | Vertically opposite | 113° |
| L6 | 152° | Linear pair | 28° |
| L7 | 47° | Vertically opposite | 47° |
| L8 | 91° | Linear pair | 89° |
| M1–M4 | random 25–155° | random | — |

Answers include Angle Hunt's traps (the known angle, 180°, 360° − answer, ±10°, double the angle).
A wrong answer's shots are harmless, so a trap only 10° off can never destroy the target by accident.

More rules are already implemented in `src/game/angles.js` and drawn by the radar (parts of a right
angle, angles around a point, angles in a triangle), ready for later levels.

## 4. Progress and rewards

- **Stars** per sector: all first-try = 3, at least 60% = 2, otherwise 1 (max 12 per run).
- **Upgrades** after each sector (pick 1 of 3): rapid cannon, shield +30, hull +30, lock-on +2 s,
  fast recharge. A new run from the station resets them.
- **Best score** is saved on the device.

## 5. Tech

- **Three.js r170** through an import map; Python `serve.py` (no-cache) for local play; Vercel for hosting.
- **Headless Blender 5.2** builds the `.glb` models (`blender/build_all.sh`).
- **Web Audio**: all sound effects and the music are synthesized (cruise / battle / boss moods).

## 6. File layout

```
index.html              page, HUD, menus, report, on-screen controls
src/main.js             boot, game loop, combat, introductions, report and upgrades
src/core/               renderer, post-processing, input, camera rig
src/world/              sky, stars, sun, planets, black hole, asteroids, portal, angle radar, holograms
src/ship/               ship, turret, engines, shield, radar ring
src/enemies/            raiders, the Warden
src/combat/             projectiles, explosions, trails
src/game/               intro (cockpit + launch + black hole), campaign (levels), angle questions,
                        quiz flow, director (introductions), banter (enemy comms lines)
src/audio/              synthesized sound and music
src/ui/                 HUD, on-screen controls, comms dialogue
assets/models/          generated .glb models
assets/ui/              title, cockpit and button art
blender/                headless Blender build scripts
```

## 7. Next ideas

- More rule sectors using the extra rules (right angle, around a point, triangle)
- A hint button (Angle Hunt has three hint levels: rule, equation, answer)
- Remember stars per level and show a level map
- Settings: difficulty (slower enemy fire), reduced motion, music volume
